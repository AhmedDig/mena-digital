Lighton Platform – Integrated LMS & CRM

Complete learning management ecosystem combining Laravel, Moodle, and a custom CRM – fully dynamic.

Project Overview

The Lighton Platform is a fully dynamic, integrated educational ecosystem that replaces the legacy static site. It combines a Laravel‑powered website and custom CRM with a Moodle e‑learning system, all connected through unified authentication and real‑time data synchronisation. Administrators manage students, enrollments, payments, and course content from a single dashboard – while students access courses, quizzes, and assignments seamlessly.

The Challenge

The academy outgrew its static site and needed:

  • A fully dynamic website with CMS‑style content management
  • A custom CRM to manage students, leads, and enrollments
  • Integration with Moodle for course delivery and assessment
  • Unified login and data synchronisation across all systems

The Solution

We built a modern, integrated platform:

  • Laravel – dynamic website, CRM, and admin dashboard
  • Moodle – fully integrated e‑learning with courses, quizzes, and assignments
  • Unified authentication – single sign‑on across both systems
  • REST APIs – real‑time sync between Laravel and Moodle
  • Advanced reporting – student progress, completion rates, and revenue
