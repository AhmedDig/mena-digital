How It All Started

In 2018, Ahmed Hammad was working with a small non-profit in Cairo. They had brilliant ideas but no technical resources to execute them. Their donor database was a mess. Their website was outdated. Their social media was sporadic.

Ahmed built them a simple CRM, automated their email campaigns, and redesigned their website in two weeks. The impact was immediate: donations increased, volunteers signed up faster, and the team could focus on their mission instead of spreadsheets.

That moment was the seed of Mena Digital.

We realized that technology shouldn't be a barrier – it should be a bridge. Since then, we've grown into a team of 8 passionate people who believe in the same thing: digital transformation that empowers.
